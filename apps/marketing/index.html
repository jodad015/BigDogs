Every Office Has Its Games

The pull-up bar by the kitchen. The putting green in the hallway. The cube timer on somebody's desk. Everyone says they hold the record, and the proof is a whiteboard that got erased.

BigDogs gives your crew shared leaderboards for any game you play. Log a score in a few taps, see who's on top, and put the board on the office TV so nobody forgets.

How It Works

1

Join Your Org

Grab an invite code from a coworker and you're in. Belong to as many groups as you like.

2

Make a Board

Pick what counts: time, reps, points, distance, or made out of attempts. Choose whether higher or lower wins.

3

Log Scores

Record your own, or log one for the coworker who swears they'll sign up later.

4

Claim the Top Spot

Standings update instantly. Look at all time, or reset the race every week or month.

Track Anything

Longest dead hang. Fastest Rubik's solve. Putts made out of 10. Cornhole doubles. If you can count it or time it, it gets a board.
